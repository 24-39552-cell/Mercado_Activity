Iconic Operator: Ghost

Lieutenant Simon "Ghost" Riley

Lieutenant Simon "Ghost" Riley was a British Special Forces operator. He was the second in command of Task Force 141 and was seen wearing dark red sunglasses and a skull-patterned balaclava along with a headset. The final mission Ghost led was a strike team to search for Makarov in his safe house, but they found intel instead and downloaded them onto a device. Ghost and Roach would then be murdered by General Shepherd after Shepherd got all the intel he needed.

  • Unit: Task Force 141
  • Primary Specialty: Recon & Special Operations
  • Signature Weapon: Custom M4 / Locus Sniper
